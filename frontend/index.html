🎓 Trading 101: A Beginner's Guide

1. Why Can't Anyone Trade with $0 Real Money?

Real crypto exchanges are like digital supermarkets: to buy Bitcoin or Ethereum, you must provide currency (US Dollars, Euros, etc.). No legitimate platform gives away free crypto to trade without collateral. That is why professional traders test their algorithms first using Paper Trading (Simulation).

2. What is Paper Trading?

Paper trading uses 100% real, live market prices, but your orders are recorded virtually in your simulated account. It allows you to learn market movements, test bot logic, and experience wins and losses without risking a single dollar.

3. What are Moving Averages (EMA)?

An Exponential Moving Average (EMA) smooths out choppy price noise. When a fast-reacting average (e.g. 9 periods) crosses above a slower average (e.g. 21 periods), it indicates rising buyer momentum (called a Golden Cross). When it crosses below, it indicates downward momentum (a Death Cross).

4. What is the Relative Strength Index (RSI)?

RSI measures speed and change of price moves on a 0 to 100 scale. Readings below 30 suggest the asset is oversold (cheap, potential bounce). Readings above 70 indicate it is overbought (expensive, potential pullback).

5. The Golden Rule of Risk Management

Never trade without a Stop-Loss. A Stop-Loss automatically exits your position if the price drops by a set percentage (e.g. 2.5%), protecting your capital so a small mistake never becomes a catastrophic loss.
